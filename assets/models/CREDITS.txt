Console models for plumsg's 3D library view
===========================================

megadrive.glb
  "Sega Mega Drive" by synthetic worlds (https://sketchfab.com/syntheticworlds),
  mesh and textures by Jon Blenky, modelled for the synthetic worlds VR gaming room
  (http://syntheticworlds.co.uk/).
  Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/sega-mega-drive-47cf3eae9fc3423db22aba119a6a37d5
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Changes: textures reduced to 2048x2048 (the colour one as JPEG) and the
  metallic/roughness texture removed, to keep the file small.

megadrive-cartridge.glb
  "Sonic 2 Mega Drive Cartridge" by Naser (https://sketchfab.com/naser.ali).
  Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/sonic-2-mega-drive-cartridge-4c453f8527384c718f652a9f04067119
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Changes: the normal map reduced to 1024x1024, both textures as JPEG, and the
  roughness and occlusion textures removed. plumsg prints each game's label (or
  its cover) over the model's own.

mastersystem.glb, mastersystem-cartridge.glb
  "Sega Master System" by dark_igorek (https://sketchfab.com/dark_igorek).
  Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/sega-master-system-0ec998c9b9de4e7f956cab56bbca3c0f
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Changes: split in two, the console with its two pads and the cartridge (the
  cables, the Sega Cards and the floor left out); textures reduced to 2048x2048
  (normal maps 1024x1024) as JPEG, the metallic/roughness textures removed.
  plumsg prints each game's label (or its cover) over the cartridge's own.

Your own models: put a .glb with the same name in the "models" folder next to
plumsg.ini and plumsg uses it instead.
